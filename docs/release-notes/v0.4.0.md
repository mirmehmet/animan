## What's new in v0.4.0

- **Long series in ranges** — episode and chapter lists over 100 entries are split into 1–100, 101–200 … ranges, and the page opens where you left off.
- **Ongoing series** — titles without a known episode or chapter count can now be tracked: anime list the episodes aired so far, and manga get a *"Read up to chapter"* box. Progress shows as `150 / ?`.
- **Safer "mark up to here"** — if it would clear later progress, you are asked first.
- **Clear error messages** — when something can't load you see why, with a *Retry* button; failed actions (adding, saving progress, changing status, restoring) show a short notice instead of failing silently.
- **Automatic library backup** — before an update changes the database, a copy of your library is saved to the `backups` folder (last 3 kept). Settings now shows when you last exported a backup.
- **Snappier pages** — Library, Dashboard and Stats only reload when something changed, so coming back from a title keeps your place; search waits until you stop typing.

## Fixes

- Data from the AniList fallback no longer overwrites MyAnimeList details; affected titles are repaired once and refresh when the main source is back.
- Episode lists now appear instantly when you're offline.
- "Delete all data" no longer reports success when it failed, and a reset is all-or-nothing.
- Import handles duplicate entries and time zones correctly, asks before replacing your library, and the app can't be closed mid-import. Notes and ratings are saved when you close the app.
- Status buttons no longer show the wrong status after a failed save; quick clicks are saved in order.
- Broken cover downloads are no longer kept as covers.
- Manga previews show manga labels and open the right MyAnimeList page; the "mark as completed" prompt no longer repeats for completed titles.

## Upgrading

- Your library is upgraded automatically; a one-time repair runs quietly on first start.
- The backup format hasn't changed: backups from v0.4.0 and v0.3.0 can be imported in either version, and older backups still import.
